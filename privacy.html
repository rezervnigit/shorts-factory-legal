Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Summary

shorts-factory is a personal tool that posts its owner's own videos to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It does not collect data about other people, has no user database and does not sell or share data.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your display name and username, used only to confirm which account a video is posted to.
  • Access and refresh tokens: used only to post the videos you choose (video.publish).

3. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored in a file on the owner's own computer. They are never uploaded to any server other than the TikTok and YouTube APIs themselves, and never shared with third parties.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not read your messages, followers, or other users' data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data.

5. Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

6. Children

shorts-factory is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
